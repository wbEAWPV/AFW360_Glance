# WP05 — Geography

- **Wave:** 2. **Depends on:** wave 1 merged, and gate G1. **Slug:** `geography`. **Acceptance script:** `pipeline/acceptance/wp05_geography.R`.

## Goal

The two geography codelists, the boundary registry, and two GeoPackages built from the legacy shapefiles.

## Read (exactly this)

- `packages/COMMON.md` and this card.
- `contract/geo_codes.csv` (35 rows; you may read it whole).
- The header rows of `CL_GEO_SCHEME.csv`, `CL_GEO.csv` and `GEO_SOURCES.csv` in `contract/csv_headers.csv`.

## Inputs

`data_raw/shp/sen_admin0.shp`, `sen_admin1.shp`, `gnb_admin0.shp` and `gnb_admin1.shp`. Use these plain layers, never the `_em` variants. The fields you need are `adm0_pcode`, `adm1_pcode`, `adm0_name`, `adm1_name`, `valid_on`, and the version field (`version` in the SEN layers, `cod_versio` in the GNB layers).

## Owned outputs

- `pipeline/bootstrap/build_geo_codelists.R` and `pipeline/bootstrap/text/geo_schemes_text.csv`
- `metadata/codelists/CL_GEO_SCHEME.csv` and `CL_GEO.csv`
- `pipeline/build_geo.R`, `pipeline/R/geo.R` and `pipeline/tests/testthat/test-geo.R`
- `geo/boundaries/SEN_CODAB_v02.gpkg`, `GNB_CODAB_V01.gpkg` and `metadata/registries/GEO_SOURCES.csv`

## Rules you need

- Spatial schemes are parallel, not nested. `nests_in` and `parent` stay empty, even though Senegal's zones happen to group its regions.
- Maps exist for countries and ADM1 only. `has_geometry` is `Y` for `ADM0` and `ADM1`, and `N` for `ZONES` and `AEZ`. A scheme without geometry has no `source_id` and no `geom_layer`.
- ADM0 codes (`SN`, `GW`) are geometry-only. They are registered in `CL_GEO` so that the outline can be drawn, but they are never used in the `GEO` column of a data row; the whole country is `GEO = _T` there. Say this in their `notes`.
- The `.prj` files declare WGS 84 without an EPSG code. Check that the coordinates are longitude and latitude, then assign EPSG:4326 (assign it, do not reproject).
- Polygon SN13 is invalid under GEOS as delivered; SN01 is invalid only under S2. Read and build with `sf::sf_use_s2(FALSE)` and apply `sf::st_make_valid()` to every layer. Validity is checked with GEOS, and both polygons must be valid in the output.
- A GeoPackage's bytes change on every build. Its sha256 is therefore recorded after the build that is committed, and a rebuild is compared by content, never by bytes.
- `valid_from` in `CL_GEO` is the boundary vintage (`valid_on` from the shapefile) for ADM0 and ADM1 units, and `TBD` for zones. It is not the period of the data.
- The three zone names truncated at 32 characters stay exactly as `geo_codes.csv` gives them, with the `TBD` remark inside the name.

## Steps

1. Write `geo_schemes_text.csv`: six rows with the columns `ref_area, code, name_en, definition_en, local_name_en, notes`. The schemes are SEN `ADM0`, `ADM1`, `ZONES`, and GNB `ADM0`, `ADM1`, `AEZ`.
2. Write `build_geo_codelists.R` (`--root`, `--out-root`). It writes two files:
   - `CL_GEO_SCHEME`, 6 rows: the text file's columns, plus `has_geometry` by the rule above, `partition = Y`, and `nests_in` empty.
   - `CL_GEO`, 35 rows, in the order of `geo_codes.csv`:
     - `code`, `name_en`, `ref_area`, `scheme`, `source_id`, `geom_layer` and `notes` come from the seed;
     - `definition_en` is generated, for example "ADM1 unit of SEN in boundary source SEN_CODAB_v02" or "ZONES unit of SEN, as used in the legacy tables";
     - `valid_from` follows the rule above, read from the shapefiles;
     - `parent` and `valid_to` stay empty.
3. Write `pipeline/R/geo.R` with the helpers `read_admin_layer(path, level)` and `make_boundary_layer(sf, ref_area, scheme)`. They keep only the fields `geo_code`, `ref_area`, `scheme` and `name`, make the geometries valid, and set the CRS.
4. Write `pipeline/build_geo.R` (`--root`, `--out-root`). For each country it:
   - writes the layers `adm0` and `adm1` to `geo/boundaries/<source_id>.gpkg`, replacing the file;
   - writes `metadata/registries/GEO_SOURCES.csv` with 2 rows: `source_id`; `ref_area`; `provider = OCHA COD-AB`; `version` and `valid_on` from the shapefile; `url` and `download_date` empty; `licence = TBD`; `attribution = TBD`; `crs = EPSG:4326`; `file` relative to `geo/boundaries/` (for example `SEN_CODAB_v02.gpkg`); `layers = adm0 adm1`; `sha256` of the file just written; `status = DRAFT`; `version_added = 0.1.0`.
5. Run both scripts, write the tests, and commit the scripts, the text file, the two codelists, the registry and the two `.gpkg` files.

## Acceptance checks

- WP05.A1: each GeoPackage has exactly the layers `adm0` and `adm1`, with 1 + 14 features for SEN and 1 + 9 for GNB (`GEOM.*` in `contract/expected_counts.csv`).
- WP05.A2: per country and layer, the `geo_code` set equals the `CL_GEO` codes of that scheme, with one feature per code. Every feature carries the right `ref_area` and `scheme`.
- WP05.A3: every layer is EPSG:4326, and every geometry is valid under GEOS (`sf_use_s2(FALSE)`).
- WP05.A4: `CL_GEO` has 35 rows whose (`code`, `ref_area`, `scheme`, `name_en`, `source_id`, `geom_layer`) equal `geo_codes.csv`. `CL_GEO_SCHEME` has the 6 rows above, with `has_geometry` by the rule.
- WP05.A5: `GEO_SOURCES` has 2 rows, and each `sha256` equals the committed file's sha256.
- WP05.A6: the headers of the three CSV files equal `csv_headers.csv`. Every **R** column is filled, and `TBD` appears only in `valid_from`, `licence` and `attribution`.
- WP05.A7: `build_geo_codelists.R --out-root <tempdir>` reproduces both codelists byte for byte. `build_geo.R --out-root <tempdir>` reproduces the same layers, `geo_code` values and feature counts, and every rebuilt feature is `st_equals_exact()` to the committed feature with the same `geo_code` at tolerance 1e-9. The GeoPackage bytes are not compared, because the format stores a timestamp.
- WP05.A8: for every unit, the polygon area in the GeoPackage is within 0.1 % of the area of the source shapefile polygon after `st_make_valid`.
